We never love anyone. What we love is the idea we have of someone. It's our own concept — our own selves — that we love.

我们从未真正爱过谁。我们爱的，是我们对某人的想象。那是我们自己的观念，是另一个自我。

Fernando Pessoa · The Book of Disquiet

The same feeling of not belonging, of futility, wherever I go: I pretend interest in what matters nothing to me, I bestir myself mechanically or out of charity, without ever being caught up, without ever being somewhere. What attracts me is elsewhere, and I don't know where that elsewhere is.

无论我走到哪里，那种格格不入、无所事事的感觉始终如一：我假装对自己毫不在意的事物有兴趣，机械地或出于善意地打起精神，却从未真正投入，从未真正在场。吸引我的总是在别处，而我不知道那别处在何方。

Emil M. Cioran · The Trouble With Being Born

生活本无意义——这恰恰是活下去的理由，唯一的理由。

Everything around me is evaporating. My whole life, my memories, my imagination and its contents, my personality — it's all evaporating. I continuously feel that I was someone else, that I felt something else, that I thought something else. What I'm attending here is a show with another set. And the show I'm attending is myself.

我周围的一切都在蒸发。我的整个人生，我的记忆，我的想象与其中的一切，我的个性——都在蒸发。我不断感到，自己曾是另一个人，曾感受过别的东西，曾思考过别的事。我此刻参与的，是另一套布景的演出。而这场演出，正是我自己。

Fernando Pessoa · The Book of Disquiet

最伤人的感受，最刺人的情绪，恰恰是那些荒诞的——对不可能之事的渴望，正因其不可能；从未存在之物的乡愁；本可以发生之事的心愿；未能成为他人的遗憾；对世界存在本身的不满。

Fernando Pessoa · The Book of Disquiet

简媜 Chien Chen · 《女儿红》 Daughter Red
